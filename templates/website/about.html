About ZILARD

Institutional profile

ZILARD is an autonomous research, education and policy analysis institute guaranteed by the Zambia Congress of Trade Unions (ZCTU) that aims to assist in building trade unions labour research, education and policy analysis capacities for better capturing and influencing national, regional and international policy-space and development.

ZILARD is legally registered under the Patents and Companies Registration Agency (PACRA) and governed by a multi-disciplinary Board of Directors drawn from academia, labour movements, public institutions, and the private sector.

Vision

The vision of ZILARD is to advance grounded evidence-based labour research, education and policy analysis that enhances a strong, dynamic and accountable trade union movement in Zambia to promote and defend socio-economic justice, fairness and equality in favour of workers.

Mission

A labour research think-tank that capacitates trade unions with labour research, education and policy analysis for effective input into national, regional and global policy discussions and actions for the benefit of the Zambian worker and the people.

Our philosophy

A holistic view of development

In defining its stake in the national, regional, continental and global political economy, ZILARD seeks to view the development process in a holistic manner where the following components are a driver to achieving worker liberation and social justice:

Social factor

Workers' and people's basic human rights are safeguarded, and how vulnerable people are protected against poverty and exploitation.

Democratic factor

The political system functions ethically and implements decisions and distributes resources and opportunities in a just and fair manner.

Global factor

Systems at the global level should respond to the needs of the workers and the people.

ZILARD stands for the redefinition of the working class ideological grinding that will use evidence-based labour research to create a critical trade union movement that understands their values and can engage in national and international solidarity. In doing so, a base should be constructed on which a parallel, joint programme of basic grassroots trade union activism can then be pursued based on trade union values of independence, worker control, bias to the poor and solidarity.

What shapes our work

Four contextual imperatives

ZILARD's research and programming are informed by four contextual imperatives:

  1. The evolving and dynamic challenges facing workers in the formal and informal economies, in Zambia and globally.
  2. Workers and trade unions as agents of change.
  3. The capacity and commitment of trade unions to bring about the change their members desire.
  4. The recognition that lasting change can only be achieved through tripartite collective bargaining and genuine social dialogue between government, employers, and workers -- at local, national, and global levels.

How we deliver our mission

Objectives

  • Initiate demand-driven labour research linked to an education agenda that refines trade union consciousness and ideological grounding.
  • Build the research capacity of trade unions to advocate for change through evidence-based policy positions for working people.
  • Strengthen research as a tool for effective education, training, and capacity building for better service delivery to members.
  • Assist in the development of empirically informed trade union policy interventions at the national, regional, and continental levels.
  • Maintain an accurate and accessible database on the economy and labour relations in Zambia.
  • Provide an online and offline platform for discussion on labour and national development issues.

Research capacity

Institutional infrastructure and resources

  • A core team of experienced researchers, including an economist, sociologist, communication specialists and field coordinators.
  • Access to a national pool of trained enumerators and community research assistants.
  • Digital data-collection tools, including ODK and KoboToolbox.
  • Analytical software licences for SPSS, NVivo, and Stata.
  • Research ethics clearance experience from the University of Zambia and NACOSTI, where required.

Institutional partnerships

  • The University of Zambia (Department of Economics and Institute of Economic and Social Research)
  • ZamStats -- for data alignment and methodology coordination
  • The Zambia Congress of Trade Unions (ZCTU)
  • Zambia Federation of Employers (ZFE)

ZILARD also maintains a repository of research outputs, accessible via its forthcoming online knowledge portal.
