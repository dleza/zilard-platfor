Who we are

An independent institute for labour research

Established in 2018, ZILARD provides evidence-based analysis, technical advice, and advocacy support to trade unions, government ministries, development agencies, and civil society organisations working to improve the conditions of work and livelihoods for Zambian workers.

Read more about ZILARD →

Our mission

To generate knowledge that empowers working people and shapes fairer economic policies, with a strong emphasis on applied research, participatory methodologies, and capacity building -- guided by social dialogue, labour rights, and democratic accountability.

Four thematic areas guide our work
